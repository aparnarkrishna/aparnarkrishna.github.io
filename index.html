Power–Distance Operating Regions of Dispersion Compensation Schemes in 10 Gbps Optical Links

Simulated a 10 Gbps optical link in OptiSystem and compared pre-compensation and post-compensation schemes across transmission power and fibre distance, using Q-factor and bit error rate to identify operating regions.
